FREE ONLINE WORD COUNTER

How many words
are in your text?

Wordly is a simple and fast online word counter designed for students, writers, bloggers, social media creators and anyone who needs to keep track of their text length.

Type or paste your text into the editor and Wordly automatically calculates your word count, character count, sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time.

There is no registration and no software to install. Everything happens instantly in your browser.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

What is a word counter? +

A word counter counts the number of words in a piece of text. Wordly also provides character, sentence, paragraph and reading time statistics.

Is Wordly free? +

Yes. Wordly is designed to provide free online writing tools without requiring an account.

Does Wordly save my text? +

The counter processes your text directly in your browser. Your text does not need to be uploaded to a server for counting.

How is reading time calculated? +

Reading time is estimated using an average reading speed of approximately 200 words per minute.
